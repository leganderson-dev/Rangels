# Household and allocation model

Status: V0.5 foundation. This document records only decisions explicitly made with the product owner.

## Agreed decisions

- Anderson and Julia share one Household allocation plan.
- Existing accounts belong to Anderson. Future accounts can be assigned to Julia or Joint.
- Joint is intended for target/shared accounts; contributions are still attributed from the source account owner.
- The account owner identifies who contributed or effectuated its transactions.
- The joint account is a target account. Transfers into it are internal movements, not income or expenses.
- Julia's business is Folium Studio. Business — ASA and Business — Folium Studio remain separate from Household.
- The Household targets are Daily Expenses 60%, Splurge 10%, Smile 10%, and Fire Extinguisher 20%.
- Percentages apply to incoming eligible income; they do not represent sequential physical accounts.
- Splurge and Smile have individual allowances for Anderson and Julia.
- Unused individual allowances roll forward.
- Allowance owners may immediately transfer accumulated allocation to another allowance, bucket, or goal.
- Allocation transfers must be immutable audit entries; corrections use reversing entries.

## Still undecided

- Whether Household allocation uses actual business distributions only or may also use declared available business income.
- How the shared Splurge and Smile targets are divided between Anderson, Julia, and shared goals.
- The detailed business allocation plans for ASA and Folium Studio.
- The detailed property plans and treatment of property distributions.
- How Fire Extinguisher allocations are divided between Mojo, debt reduction, mortgages, and future goals.
- Whether planned declarations are required in V1 or deferred.

## V0.5 scope

V0.5 introduces household members, account ownership, the Household context, Folium Studio, and a persisted shared allocation plan. It deliberately does not invent answers for the undecided items above.
