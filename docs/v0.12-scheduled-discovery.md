# Rangels V0.12 — Scheduled transaction discovery

V0.12 analyses classified, non-transfer transaction history for recurring merchant patterns.

Suggestions require at least three occurrences and are ranked using:

- consistency of the interval between transactions;
- stability of the transaction amount; and
- the amount of supporting history.

The detector recognises daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly and yearly periods. A suggestion shows its merchant, inferred period, typical amount, observed range, next expected date, confidence and recent evidence. Existing schedules for the same merchant, account and direction are excluded.

Nothing is created automatically. Reviewing a suggestion opens the ordinary Scheduled Transaction form, pre-populated with the most recent Purpose, Context, Household Bucket and Tax treatment. The owner can amend every field before saving.

Suggestions are grouped by Purpose and support individual, group-level and all-results selection. Deleted suggestions are stored as owner-scoped dismissals, so they remain hidden after reanalysis without deleting or changing their supporting transaction history.

Transfers remain excluded from automatic discovery because recurring transfers require explicit source, destination and transfer-reason review.
